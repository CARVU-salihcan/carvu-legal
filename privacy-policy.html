Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

carVu ("we," "our," or "us") operates the carVu mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: When you register or sign in using Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address, and a unique user identifier from those services.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the App, including vehicle searches, screens visited, features used, and session duration.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and push notification tokens.
  • Vehicle request data: Vehicle make, model, and year information you submit when requesting a vehicle to be added to our database.
  • User Content: If you create a community post, we collect and store user-generated content you choose to publish, including free-text descriptions, author display name, category, recurrence, optional mileage/repair cost, and related vehicle details, together with your account identifier, so other users can read ownership experiences in the App.
  • Purchase information: Subscription and purchase history processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store your payment card details.
  • Crash and error data: Technical error reports and crash logs to help us identify and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App and its features
  • Personalize your experience and remember your preferences
  • Process subscription purchases and manage your account
  • Send push notifications about vehicle updates you have requested (you may opt out at any time in your device settings)
  • Send re-engagement notifications if you have not used the App for an extended period (you may opt out at any time)
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve the App
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Use anonymous analytics and aggregated usage data to improve the accuracy of vehicle risk analysis
  • Host and moderate community posts that you choose to publish

3. Ownership Verdict (On-Device Analysis)

The App may show an Ownership Verdict. This feature generates a plain-language ownership recommendation from vehicle issue data already loaded in the App.

  • How it is produced: The verdict is calculated on your device using deterministic, rule-based logic over our vehicle issue database (known patterns, categories, severity, and related ownership signals for the selected configuration).
  • Where it runs: The calculation runs on-device. Unlocking the verdict (for example via Pro subscription or a rewarded ad) only removes an in-app access gate; it does not send the recommendation request to a generative AI model.
  • No third-party generative AI: For the Ownership Verdict feature, carVu does not send your personal data—such as your name, email address, account identifier, authentication token, device identifier, free-text prompts, or community content—to a third-party generative AI / large language model provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini).
  • Separate from other processing: This section describes only the Ownership Verdict feature. Other App features may still send data to our backend and to the third-party service providers listed in Section 5 (for example authentication, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, and crash reporting). Those practices are described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy and are separate from Ownership Verdict generation.

4. Third-Party AI Services

carVu does not use third-party artificial intelligence or large language model (LLM) services to generate Ownership Verdicts or to process your personal data.

  • We do not integrate providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or similar generative AI APIs.
  • We do not share personal data—including account identifiers, email addresses, authentication tokens, device identifiers, free-text prompts, community User Content, or vehicle selections—with any third-party AI / LLM provider.
  • The Ownership Verdict is computed on your device using fixed, deterministic rules applied to vehicle issue data already loaded in the App. No generative model call is required to produce the verdict.

To opt out of interest-based advertising, you can adjust your device's ad tracking settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • Protection of rights: We may disclose information to protect the rights, property, or safety of carVu, our users, or the public.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or legitimate business purposes.
